Starting Day 18 of learning Claude AI

What I learnt : Custom Skills allow Claude to remember complex workflows. Instead of manually organizing notes every time, you can create a reusable skill that automatically transforms unstructured information into project dashboards, action plans, and decision logs.

1
Reusable Workflows: Create once and use forever.
2
Information Management: Convert messy notes into structured outputs.
3
Project Planning: Generate action plans, risks, and decisions automatically.
4
Productivity: Reduce manual organization work significantly.
