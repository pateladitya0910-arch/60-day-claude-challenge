Day 10 of learning Claude AI

What I learnt: Modern portfolios are no longer limited to developers. Using Claude, anyone can generate beautiful personal websites showcasing skills, projects, achievements, and professional profiles. This helps build credibility, visibility, and personal branding.

1
Personal Branding: Create a strong online professional presence.
2
AI Web Development: Generate websites without writing code manually.
3
Portfolio Building: Showcase projects, skills, and achievements.
4
Career Growth: Improve visibility among recruiters and hiring managers.
